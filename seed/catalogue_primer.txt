DO NOT OFFER
  labels: static cling will not hold on textured or porous walls; the electrostatic grip drops and the edge lifts
  banners: car-window clings must not sit in the driver's direct line of sight (safety, not capability)
  labels: front-adhesive decals are refused on textured or porous surfaces; the adhesive needs a clean flat surface
  labels: vinyl front-adhesive is one-time application; it loses its stick once removed, so reuse is not offered
  labels: sticky vinyl decals are for long-term application and are not re-usable; only static cling is
  labels: paper stock labels are not made for water or weather; wet use needs a synthetic stock
  labels: we will not print a food label on stock that cannot withstand cold storage, oil or handling
  boxes: open-front display boxes are shelf units and are not durable for heavy shipping
  boxes: paperboard alone does not control temperature; coatings only block moisture
  boxes: precision foam inserts suit uneven bottles but the page warns they hurt the pack's recyclability
  business_cards: foil runs under 1,000 are refused for machine set-up, not price: print 1,000 and split the batch
  bags: foil-lined structures are multi-layer and some recycling streams reject them, so no curbside claim
  bags: thin film cannot hold a deep debossed impression; the hub limits it to thicker bags
  bags: flexographic printing is refused for short runs and for designs that keep changing (plate cost)
  bags: flexographic printing is not quoted for photo-level sharp detail
  bags: a press-to-close zipper is not sold as a replacement for the original heat-seal barrier
  bags: a standard sealed pouch is unsuitable for products that keep releasing gas (coffee) unless a degassing valve is added
  bags: not every vacuum pouch is heat-safe; the hub says to check the product specification
  banners: the wrinkle-free fabric sign grade is explicitly not recommended for exterior UV and rain
  boxes: pillow boxes are described as carrying small, light goods only, not heavy items
  boxes: a seal-end box is not meant for products the customer opens and closes repeatedly
  boxes: duplex board is ruled out for very heavy or delicate products
  boxes: CCNB is not offered for heavy-duty use
  boxes: lined paperboard is not supported for heavy products
  boxes: chipboard cannot take very heavy weight; it is a shape-and-structure stock
  print: construction paper is ruled out for detailed printing; the surface is rough
  never ours, anywhere: pillow box, sleeve box, rigid mylar box, Child-Guard zipper, squeeze-then-slide zipper, braille embossing, letterpress
