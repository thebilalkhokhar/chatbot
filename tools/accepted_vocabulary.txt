# Accepted kb_v2 vocabulary — the review queue output of tools/extract_capabilities.py.
#
# Every line is a value that kb_v2 product pages print verbatim under their own `## Materials`,
# `## Finishes` or `## Add-ons` heading, and that the curated registry vocabulary did not yet name.
# Format: `slot: canonical form | written variant | written variant` — variants are how the site
# spells the same value; the canonical form is what the registry cell uses.
#
# Not accepted, on purpose:
#   - gauge and capacity figures ("13 PT", "70 lbs to 100 lbs", "1 oz"): those are `gauge`/`sizes`
#     rows, not vocabulary;
#   - bare colour words ("Clear", "White", "Silver", "Frosted"): they sit under `## Materials` on
#     the site but they are not substrates, so they get their own `colour` slot and stay out of the
#     capability tuple;
#   - marketing qualifiers ("premium", "high quality", "eco friendly", "more options",
#     "customizable based on requirements") and product-form words ("Stickers", "Clear Bags"):
#     they name no material;
#   - compliance wording ("FDA-approved virgin chipboard", "puncture resistant", "uv resistant"):
#     that belongs to compliance-allowlist.md, never to a vocabulary cell.
